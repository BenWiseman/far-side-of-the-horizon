Note on the source's b_90 formula: sec 21 writes 'b_90 = G M1 m_N / v^2', which is
dimensionally inconsistent as a length once M1 and m_N are both taken as literal
masses (units of cm*g, not cm) and numerically wrong by ~24 orders of magnitude if
evaluated that way. The standard two-body Newtonian-scattering formula for a light
target deflected by a much heavier source (M1 = 4.8e8 GeV >> m_N ~ 1 GeV) is
b_90 = G*M_heavy/v^2, independent of the light mass; that reproduces the quoted
1.1e-37 cm to 8%, so that is what is implemented above -- flagged, not silently fixed.
